# fitted. — Content System · Final Edition
**The definitive production reference for all social media content**
*Consolidates and supersedes v2.0 and v2.1*

---

## SECTION 1: DESIGN DIRECTION

### The Hierarchy

Three creative references. Always applied in this order.

---

**1 · Eszter Bial — The Dominant Register**

Everything begins here. Bial means: airy elegance, intentional negative space, personal warmth that never tips into sentimentality, and content that feels in conversation rather than performing at you. Her work feels like a beautiful thought left on a well-lit table. That's the fitted. feed.

In practice:
- Every cover has one dominant element — a word, a number, a question, a sentence. Never two competing ideas.
- Whitespace is load-bearing. It creates emphasis. When a layout feels crowded, remove something rather than adjusting size.
- Holds in Reels are long enough to breathe — 5–7 seconds on the hook scene. The viewer should have time to feel the thing before the next thought arrives.
- Interactivity reads as genuine curiosity, not a tactic. "I actually want to know" over "drop a comment below."
- Stories feel like a quiet check-in from someone who respects your time.
- The goal: every post should feel worth saving even if you're not currently job searching.

The test: *Does this feel made by a human with a specific point of view — or does it feel like content?*

---

**2 · Monocle — The Editorial Backbone**

Monocle brings calm intelligence and the confidence that every word was chosen. Structure without rigidity. Typography that makes an argument, not just decoration. The editorial voice that makes fitted.'s insights feel trustworthy rather than advisory.

In practice:
- Data is stated without hedging. Numbers appear without softening.
- Carousel slides read like a well-edited short article — one idea per slide, no filler, no repetition.
- Captions make a single point, then stop.
- The "fitted. thinks:" device, used sparingly, functions like a section header in a sharp magazine.

The test: *Is this worth keeping? Would someone screenshot this slide?*

---

**3 · Studio Yoke — Used as Punctuation Only**

Yoke's minimalism is used in exactly two contexts: EP pricing close slides, and AC score-reveal moments. Nowhere else. The single greatest risk in this system is Yoke creeping into the overall register and making the feed feel dark, remote, or cold. Quiet is the goal. Lifeless is the failure.

The test: *If I added one warm element here, would it improve? If yes — add it.*

---

### Non-Negotiables

- Nothing looks like an ad. If a post could be mistaken for a promoted product post, revise until it reads as a thoughtful editorial contribution.
- Labels (SI, SE, EP, etc.) are internal production references only. They never appear as visible badges on grid posts.
- "fitted. thinks:" is acceptable as a very small, tracked DM Sans label on SI and AQ posts only — where it reads as a personal editorial note, not a category tag.
- The feed is warm and alive. Every week, check the 9-post grid view: if dark backgrounds cluster, add off-white and taupe posts to the queue.

---

### TARGET AUDIENCE — Women 22–35

**Core demographic:** Women aged 22–35 — early career, career changers, and returners.

**The voice toward this audience:** Smart big sister. The friend who has been through it and will tell you the honest version — not the comfortable one. Warm without being soft. Intelligent without being patronizing. Aware of the structural stuff and not pretending it doesn't exist — but never using it as a ceiling. Names problems once, precisely, then moves to what's actionable.

**Topics and tensions that land with this audience:**

- **Career gaps framed as data, not apologies.** Parental leave, caregiving, burnout exits, time taken for family — these are facts about someone's timeline, not character flaws. The content never treats a gap as something to explain away; it treats it as something to frame accurately.
- **The confidence attribution error.** This audience is consistently told the problem is their confidence. More often, the problem is missing information, unclear targeting, an ATS mismatch, or a title/scope gap that's invisible until someone names it. fitted. is built on this reframe.
- **Title-to-scope gaps.** Doing director-level work on a manager title. Being called "high potential" for three years without the promotion. Running a function without owning the title. These are common patterns — and they produce resumes that undersell the actual level of work. The content names this pattern specifically and shows what to do about it.
- **Values alignment and culture fit as real optimization variables.** Not soft preferences. Not "wanting too much." Legitimate strategic considerations that affect whether someone will stay and whether they'll thrive. Treated as such.
- **The translation problem.** Women who have done exceptional work in roles without prestige markers — nonprofit, education, caregiving roles, hybrid functions — often don't know how to translate that experience into language the search values. The content helps with that translation without condescending about why it's needed.
- **Negotiation as information, not confrontation.** The framing that makes negotiation less fraught: it's a calibration exercise, not a confrontation. The content can address this matter-of-factly.
- **Work-life fit as a real factor in search strategy.** Treated as a legitimate variable in targeting decisions — not a compromise or an apology.

**What doesn't land with this audience:**
- Lean-in energy ("sit at the table," "speak up," "just be more confident")
- Hustle culture re-skinned as empowerment
- Generic confidence boosts without specific tools
- Assuming the reader needs permission to want what she wants
- Treating systemic challenges as either non-existent or insurmountable

**The test:** Would a 27-year-old woman who is smart, slightly skeptical of career content, and actively searching save this post — or keep scrolling? If she'd keep scrolling, revise.

## SECTION 2: GLOBAL PRODUCTION SPECIFICATIONS

### Canvas Sizes

| Format | Dimensions | Safe zone |
|--------|------------|-----------|
| Feed Carousel (Square) | 1080 × 1080 px | 80px from all edges |
| Reel / Story (Vertical) | 1080 × 1920 px | 100px from sides, 160px top, 220px bottom |
| LinkedIn (optional) | 1200 × 627 px | 80px from all edges |

### Typography

| Role | Font | Size (Carousel) | Size (Reel) | Weight |
|------|------|----------------|-------------|--------|
| Primary headline | DM Serif Display | 44–56px | 48–64px | 400 (regular) |
| Intimate quote / accent | Cormorant Garamond Italic | 34–46px | 40–56px | 400–500 |
| Body / slide copy | DM Sans | 16–20px | 18–22px | 300–400 |
| Small label / tag | DM Sans | 11–13px | 12–14px | 400, tracked +150 |
| Large data number | DM Serif Display | 80–120px | 96–140px | 400 |

**Rule:** DM Serif Display and Cormorant Garamond Italic never share the same slide as co-equal elements. One leads; the other supports.

### Pattern & Texture Rules (updated Final)

The wave/squiggle pattern is **optional**. Use it only when it genuinely enhances the warmth or editorial texture of that specific post. Do not apply it as a default.

When to use:
- Subtle, full-bleed at 10–18% opacity — texture, not decoration
- Most appropriate on: TS, BtS, IM — the warmer, more personal types
- Navy waves on light backgrounds (`#f4f2ed`, `#b8a99a`, `#d9cfc7`)
- Off-white or pale waves on dark backgrounds (`#1a1a1f`, `#2f3e5c`, `#5171bf`)

When to omit:
- SI posts where the ink background + typography alone create sufficient depth
- AC score-reveal slides (the number should command the full frame)
- EP pricing close slides (Yoke precision — nothing competes)
- Any slide where adding texture would reduce legibility or feel busy

**Alternative motifs:** A single thin horizontal rule (taupe on light, off-white on dark), a faint typographic repeat in the bg, or a bare, clean field with intentional empty space are all valid and often stronger than the wave.

### First-Slide Color Rule

The first slide of every Carousel and the cover/thumbnail of every Reel **must use the exact main background hex assigned to that content type.**

| Content Type | Cover Hex |
|---|---|
| SI | `#1a1a1f` Ink |
| SE | `#b8a99a` Taupe |
| TS | `#2f3e5c` Navy |
| BtS | `#f4f2ed` Off-white |
| EP | `#5171bf` Periwinkle Blue |
| AQ | `#d9cfc7` Pale Taupe |
| DP | `#2f3e5c` Navy |
| AC | `#f4f2ed` Off-white |
| IM | `#b8a99a` Taupe |

### Subsequent Slide / Scene Variation

**This is where the calendar comes alive.** After the cover, introduce color, layout, and typographic variation to keep the viewer moving. An all-navy carousel feels like a wall. A carousel that breathes between slides feels like reading something worth finishing.

Recommended contrast pairings:

| Cover color | Contrast slide options |
|-------------|----------------------|
| Ink `#1a1a1f` | Deep navy `#2f3e5c` / Off-white `#f4f2ed` close |
| Taupe `#b8a99a` | Navy `#2f3e5c` (contrast), Pale taupe `#d9cfc7` (breath) |
| Navy `#2f3e5c` | Off-white `#f4f2ed` (high contrast), Taupe `#b8a99a` (warm) |
| Off-white `#f4f2ed` | Pale taupe `#ede8e3` (subtle shift), Taupe `#b8a99a` (warmth) |
| Blue `#5171bf` | Off-white `#faf9f6` (legibility slides), Navy (close) |
| Pale Taupe `#d9cfc7` | Taupe `#b8a99a` (deepen), Off-white (lighten) |

**Rule:** At least one slide shift per carousel. Never more than 2 consecutive slides with the same background.

For Reels: Scene background can vary between slides using bg color cards as transitions. Not every scene needs a different color — but a shift at Scene 3 or 4 gives visual rhythm and signals a new idea arriving.

### Motion (Reels)

- **Default motion:** Text fades in slowly — opacity 0 → 1 over 0.6s, slight upward translate (12px → 0). Feels like a thought surfacing, not a slide animating.
- **No bounce, spring, overshoot, or zoom burst.** These break the editorial register entirely.
- **Scene transitions:** Clean cut or slow dissolve (0.3s). Never swipe, slide, or flip.
- **Hover/idle on cover:** Subtle vignette pulse or no motion. The image itself is the motion.
- **Music timing:** The first VO word or text reveal should land on a musical breath, not a beat.

## SECTION 3: CONTENT TYPE COMPENDIUM

Full production spec for all 9 types.

### TYPE 1 — SI · Something Inspiring / Thoughtful
**Highlight Bucket:** Insight

---

**Tone:** The most meditative content type. Ink background creates a late-night intimacy — the feeling of thinking clearly about something most people rush past. No coaching register. No "here's what to do." A declarative observation that earns its weight. The viewer should feel seen, not instructed.

**Topics with particular resonance for women 22–35:**
- The job search is not a confidence problem — it's an information problem (the confidence attribution error, stated directly)
- Title history vs. the thesis that connects it (especially for women whose titles have lagged their actual scope)
- What "qualified" actually means vs. the 100% requirements trap
- The invisible work problem: contributions that are real but don't have prestige markers attached
- What a good culture fit actually looks like — and how to tell before you're already in
- The difference between a career gap and a career pause (the framing shift)

**Vibe:** A considered thought, held lightly. Clair de Lune at 11pm.

---

**COVER / REEL SCENE 1 SPEC (1080 × 1920px)**

```
Background: #1a1a1f (Ink)
Pattern: None, or faint off-white organic grain at 8% opacity — test both, use the cleaner version

Headline: DM Serif Display, off-white #f4f2ed, 52–60px
Position: Centered horizontally, vertically in upper 55% of frame
Max 2 lines. If 2 lines: line-height 1.2, generous tracking.

Accent (optional): Cormorant Garamond Italic, taupe #b8a99a, 30–34px
Position: 24px below headline. Use for a secondary thought only — not a subhead.

Label (if used): "fitted. thinks:" DM Sans, taupe #b8a99a, 11px, tracked +180
Position: 40px above headline. Thin taupe rule 12px below label, before headline.
Only use on SI — and only when the thought genuinely reads as an editorial voice, not a product claim.

fitted. wordmark: Off-white, centered, bottom — 140px from bottom edge.
Size: 18–22px (small, unobtrusive)

Negative space: Minimum 120px above headline, minimum 80px below accent to wordmark.
```

**Subsequent Reel scenes:**
- Scenes 2–4 can stay ink or shift to deep navy `#2f3e5c`
- Use DM Sans body copy in off-white for the detail scenes — smaller, DM Serif for Scene 1 only
- Scene 4 (close) option: bare ink background, Cormorant Garamond Italic taupe, single closing line
- No pattern on detail scenes — clean fields only

**Caption style:** One declarative sentence. No hashtag before the period. Ends with `✦` and the CTA line on a new line.

**Stories:** Pull the strongest line from Scene 2 or 3 as a standalone card. Ink bg, DM Serif Display off-white, centered. No poll — let it sit.

---

### TYPE 2 — SE · Something Educational / Reframe
**Highlight Bucket:** Clarity

---

**Tone:** Editorial intelligence in service of the reader. SE posts are short, well-edited articles — each slide advances rather than repeating. The tone is factual, calm, slightly surprising. The frame: "here's what's actually happening" rather than "here's what you should do."

**Vibe:** A Monocle feature brief. Confident, clean, worth reading twice.

---

**COVER SPEC (1080 × 1080px)**

```
Background: #b8a99a (Taupe)
Pattern: Optional — navy wave at 12% opacity, or bare. Bare often stronger.

Headline: DM Serif Display, navy #2f3e5c, 46–54px
Position: Left-aligned, 80px left margin, vertically centered in upper 50% of frame
Max 2 lines.

Subtitle (optional): Cormorant Garamond Italic, off-white #f4f2ed, 28–32px
Position: 20px below headline

Swipe prompt: "Swipe →" DM Sans, navy, 13px, lower right, 80px from edges. Subtle.

fitted. wordmark: Navy, bottom-right, 80px from edges. 16–18px.
```

**Subsequent slides (1080 × 1080px):**
- Alternate between: Taupe bg (same as cover) for narrative continuity, and Navy `#2f3e5c` bg with off-white text for high-contrast emphasis slides
- Layout: Body copy in DM Sans off-white or navy 16–18px, left-aligned, 80px margins, max 5 lines per slide
- Pull-quote slides: Cormorant Garamond Italic, centered, 36px — use when one slide carries a single powerful line
- Close slide: Off-white `#f4f2ed` bg, navy DM Serif Display, one closing line + fitted. wordmark. Feels like a breath after the argument.

**Caption style:** Reframe first ("The resume isn't a history document — it's a brief."), then one supporting line, then CTA.

**Threads:** The reframe in 2–3 sentences. No preamble.

### TYPE 3 — TS · Tell a Story / Customer Story
**Highlight Bucket:** Reflections

---

**Tone:** The warmest type. A specific person, a specific moment, handled with care. Bial entirely. The story should feel held — like a conversation someone trusted you enough to share. No triumphant resolution. The ending is accurate, grounded, true.

**For the core audience (women 22–35), rich story archetypes:**
- The returner who assumed the gap was the problem — and discovered it wasn't
- The "high potential" who was given stretch assignments for years but never the title or the promotion to match
- The career changer whose pivot failed on translation, not capability
- The woman who left a toxic or low-ceiling culture and had to rebuild her professional narrative after
- The early-career candidate who kept her search too broad to feel safe, and what finally made it click
- The senior woman whose scope was director-level but whose title said manager — and what changed when she made that visible

All TS characters are composite. Never named. The story is recognized — not admired.

**Vibe:** Warm conversation over coffee. Coffee — beabadoobee.

---

**COVER / SCENE 1 SPEC (1080 × 1920px)**

```
Background: #2f3e5c (Navy)
Pattern: Optional — off-white wave at 12% opacity. Or bare. Test both.

Primary text: Cormorant Garamond Italic, off-white #f4f2ed, 48–58px
Position: Centered, vertically in upper 55%
1–2 lines only. This is the hook — one thought.

No label. No badge. No "TELL A STORY" tag.

fitted. wordmark: Off-white, bottom-center, 140px from bottom. Small (18px).
```

**Subsequent scenes:**
- Scenes 2–3: Navy bg, DM Sans off-white body copy 18–20px. Left-aligned, centered vertically. One paragraph per scene.
- Scene 3 or 4 (optional breath): Taupe `#b8a99a` bg with navy DM Sans — creates a warm contrast moment as the story turns
- Scene 4 (close): Return to navy. Cormorant Garamond Italic taupe for the final line. No triumphant phrasing.

**"Have you ever…" openers:** Welcome and often correct. Bring the viewer into the story before the character appears.

**fitted. integration:** Appears at the natural story turning point — never as the premise or in Scene 1.

**Caption style:** Personal, grounded. One or two sentences that capture the honest truth of the story. No paraphrase of the entire Reel.

**Stories:** Hook line only, navy bg, Cormorant Garamond Italic off-white. No poll — let it sit one day.

### TYPE 4 — BtS · Behind the Scenes
**Highlight Bucket:** Craft

---

**Tone:** The founder's voice. Reflective, honest, forward-facing without being resolved. Building in public — not the triumphant version, the actual version. Bial almost completely. The person is the label.

**Vibe:** A thoughtful newsletter entry from someone who respects your intelligence.

---

**COVER / SCENE 1 SPEC (1080 × 1920px)**

```
Background: #f4f2ed (Off-white)
Pattern: None, or the faintest taupe grain at 6% opacity — never a wave on BtS

Primary text: DM Serif Display, navy #2f3e5c, 48–56px
OR: DM Sans medium, navy, 22–26px, for a more conversational register

Position: Left-aligned or centered, upper 50% of frame
1–2 lines. Scene 1 is the thought, not the title.

No label. No "BEHIND THE BUILD" badge.

fitted. wordmark: Navy, bottom-center, small.
```

**Subsequent scenes:**
- Scenes 2–3: Off-white bg, DM Sans navy body copy. Left-aligned, conversational. Can introduce a taupe `#b8a99a` bg at Scene 3 for warmth.
- Scene 4 (close): Taupe bg, DM Serif Display navy, quiet forward-facing line. Not triumphant — present-tense and honest.

**Caption style:** First person. Sounds like the person who built this. One grounded observation, then the URL.

### TYPE 5 — EP · Elegant Promotion
**Highlight Bucket:** Presence

---

**Tone:** Monocle structure, Bial warmth. The offer is explained with the confidence of someone who believes in what they built and wants you to understand it — not buy it. EP covers the full range: Founding Members, Pro, resume slot pricing, feature spotlights, tips and tricks, "what Pro unlocks" use cases.

The single test: *would a person save this post even if they weren't currently job searching?* If yes, it's working.

**Vibe:** Considered editorial. The Financial Times recommending something it genuinely thinks is worth your attention.

---

**COVER SPEC (1080 × 1080px — Carousel, most common)**

```
Background: #5171bf (Periwinkle Blue)
Pattern: Optional — very faint pale blue motif at 10% opacity (#7a95d4), or bare

Headline: DM Serif Display, off-white #faf9f6, 48–56px
Position: Centered, upper 55%
1–2 lines — the useful insight or capability, not the product name

Subtitle (optional): Cormorant Garamond Italic, pale taupe #d9cfc7, 28–32px
Position: 20px below headline

Swipe prompt: DM Sans, pale taupe, 12px, lower right

fitted. wordmark: Off-white, bottom-center, small
```

**Subsequent slides:**
- Slides 2–4: Off-white `#faf9f6` bg with navy `#2f3e5c` text — dramatically more legible for body copy. This shift from blue → off-white is intentional: the cover creates the mood, the interior does the explaining.
- Feature spotlight slides: One feature per slide. DM Sans navy body, 16–18px. Left-aligned. What it is, how it works, who it helps.
- Use-case slides: Cormorant Garamond Italic for the scenario line, DM Sans for the explanation beneath.
- Pricing close slide **(Studio Yoke precision):**
  ```
  Background: #5171bf (return to blue)
  Pattern: None
  
  Price: DM Serif Display, off-white, 80–96px, centered
  Billing period: DM Sans, pale taupe, 16px, directly below
  Thin pale taupe rule: 20px below billing line
  fitted. wordmark: off-white, centered, 40px below rule
  Nothing else.
  ```

**For feature spotlights and tips — Reel format is often better than carousel:**
- Scene 1: The useful thing, stated as a specific, surprising insight
- Scene 2: How it works (show the screen or describe the action)
- Scene 3: What changes as a result — the concrete outcome
- Scene 4: CTA, quiet

**Caption style:** The useful thing first. Then the logic. Then the URL. Never "Check out our Pro plan."

### TYPE 6 — AQ · Ask a Question
**Highlight Bucket:** Space

---

**Tone:** Bial entirely. Open, genuinely curious, not a trick with a product reveal. The question surfaces a tension the audience already feels but hasn't named. The viewer's pause is part of the experience — the comments are the content. Nothing is answered in the post.

**Feature prompt:** Include in AQ Reels, Scene 4: *"Stay until the end — we feature real answers on our channel."* Genuine, not mechanical.

**Demo resumes:** When real user data isn't available, anchor "fitted. thinks:" examples on plausible demo resumes — specific function, specific role, specific gap. Never generic. Examples calibrated to the core audience: e.g., "senior marketing manager applying to VP roles," "nonprofit program director pivoting to tech operations," "operations lead returning after parental leave."

**Questions that resonate with women 22–35 (use as starting points):**
- "Have you ever stayed in a role longer than you knew you should — and what finally made it clear?"
- "What's the one thing about your experience that you can never figure out how to put on a resume?"
- "Has anyone ever told you that you just needed to be 'more confident'? What was actually going on?"
- "What would your ideal next role give you that your current one doesn't?"
- "If you could see your resume the way a hiring manager sees it — what do you think they'd miss?"
- "What does 'the right culture' mean to you — and how do you actually tell before you're in it?"

**Vibe:** A question left on an open notebook. The silence is intentional.

---

**COVER / SCENE 1 SPEC (1080 × 1920px)**

```
Background: #d9cfc7 (Pale Taupe)
Pattern: Optional — the question alone is usually sufficient. If texture: very faint navy detail at 8% opacity.

Primary text: DM Serif Display, navy #2f3e5c, 46–54px
Position: Centered, vertically centered in upper 60% of frame
1–2 lines. The question is the entire visual.

No label. No badge.

fitted. wordmark: Navy, bottom-center, small.

Negative space: Generous above and below. The question should feel isolated — like it's the only thing in the room.
```

**Subsequent scenes:**
- Scene 2: Pale taupe bg continues, DM Sans navy body — why this specific question
- Scene 3: Can shift to taupe `#b8a99a` bg for a warmer, slightly different visual register — the texture of the problem
- Scene 4 (close + feature prompt): Return to pale taupe. DM Sans navy, body size. CTA line. Feature prompt line. Quiet.

**"fitted. thinks:" demo moment** (optional Scene 3 insert for early posts):
```
Background: Pale taupe OR very clean off-white
Small DM Sans tracked label: "fitted. thinks:" taupe, 11px
DM Serif Display navy, 38–44px: The specific insight on the demo resume
One line. Nothing else on the slide.
```

**Caption style:** The question, once, exactly as asked. One supporting sentence if needed. CTA. Never explain the question.

**Stories:** The question alone on the cover bg. Poll should offer two genuine, plausible options — not "yes/no" shortcuts.

### TYPE 7 — DP · Data Point / Quiet Truth
**Highlight Bucket:** Insight

---

**Tone:** Monocle intelligence, Bial warmth in framing. The data is stated with confidence — no hedging, no "interestingly." But the framing is human: the number is a lens, not a verdict. When strong data isn't available, shift format freely — quote carousel, word/definition reel, single-image post, contrast observation.

**Data angles with particular resonance for the core audience:**
- The 100% requirements trap: women statistically apply to roles only when they meet 100% of criteria; men apply at 60%. The cost of that gap.
- Career gap timelines: the data on how long returners' searches take compared to non-returners — and what actually shortens them.
- Title/scope divergence: how often women are performing at the level above their title, and what that does to their resume's match score.
- Language precision: the difference in callback rates between resumes with hedging verbs ("assisted with," "contributed to") and agency verbs ("rebuilt," "led," "designed").
- The pivot timeline: what actually shortens a career pivot from 12 months to 6 — spoiler: it's not trying harder.

**Vibe:** A Monocle data brief. Precise, confident, worth reading twice.

---

**COVER SPEC (1080 × 1080px — most common)**

```
Background: #2f3e5c (Navy)
Pattern: Optional — off-white wave or organic detail at 12% opacity, or bare

Headline / primary: DM Serif Display, off-white #f4f2ed, 44–56px
OR for data posts: Large number in DM Serif Display, 80–120px, centered — the number IS the cover
Position: Centered, upper 50%

Subtitle / qualifier: DM Sans, taupe #cbbdaf, 14–16px, tracked — below headline
"DATA POINT / QUIET TRUTH" label: optional, DM Sans tracked, taupe, 11px, above headline — only if it reads as editorial rather than administrative

Swipe prompt: Lower right, DM Sans taupe 12px

fitted. wordmark: Off-white, bottom-right, small
```

**Subsequent slides:**
- Slide 2: Navy bg continues — introduce the finding, DM Sans off-white body
- Slides 3–4: Can shift to off-white `#f4f2ed` bg with navy text for readability-heavy slides (lists of statistics, structured comparisons)
- Quote slides: Cormorant Garamond Italic, off-white or pale taupe, centered, 38–44px — large pull-quote on navy feels authoritative
- Close slide: Return to navy. One clean line. fitted. wordmark. No summary.

**Quote carousel format:** All 5 slides can stay on navy — but vary between DM Serif Display (authoritative) and Cormorant Garamond Italic (intimate) to create visual rhythm. Alternate which font leads per slide.

**Word/definition format (Reel):**
```
Scene 1: The word only. Cormorant Garamond Italic, large, centered. 5-second hold.
Scene 2: What most people mean when they use it
Scene 3: What fitted. / hiring managers / the market means — the reframe
Scene 4: CTA, quiet
```

**Single-image post format:**
```
Background: #2f3e5c, no pattern
Number: DM Serif Display, off-white, 96–120px, centered
One-word qualifier: DM Sans, taupe, 16px, below — e.g., "seconds" or "applications"
Thin taupe rule: below qualifier
fitted. wordmark: centered, below rule
Nothing else.
Caption does all the explanation work.
```

### TYPE 8 — AC · AI Career Coach Spotlight
**Highlight Bucket:** Clarity

---

**Tone:** Monocle editorial intelligence, Bial warmth in the human story beneath. AC posts show the AI read — specific, honest, calibrated. Never "fitted. can help." Always: here's the specific thing it saw, here's what changed. Post-launch: draw from real or plausible user sessions.

**Vibe:** A precise, honest read from someone who is very good at this — and warmly on your side.

---

**COVER / SCENE 1 SPEC (1080 × 1920px)**

```
Background: #f4f2ed (Off-white)
Pattern: Optional — navy wave at 10% opacity, or bare

Primary text: DM Serif Display, navy #2f3e5c, 48–58px
Position: Centered, upper 55%
The surprising finding or score contrast — e.g., "62% → 91%" or "Your scope is hidden."

"AI CAREER COACH SPOTLIGHT" label: Optional, DM Sans tracked, taupe #b8a99a, 11px
Only use if it reads as editorial section header, not as a category badge
Position: 36px above headline

Accent line: Cormorant Garamond Italic, taupe, 28–32px, 20px below headline
The specific, honest sub-note

fitted. wordmark: Navy, bottom-center, small
```

**Score reveal scenes (Studio Yoke precision):**
```

Pattern: None
Score: DM Serif Display, 100–130px, centered
Before score (muted): 50% opacity / taupe color
After score (full): Off-white or navy full, bold

```

**Subsequent scenes:**
- Scene 2: Off-white bg continues, DM Sans navy body — the specific gap identified

- SI covers (ink alone creates the atmosphere — texture dilutes it)
